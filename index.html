Ratatouille

the story

Ratatouille is a movie about a rat named Remy who dreams of making a change in the world and works with a down on his luck man named Linguini to work in a restaurant. They go through family and job related problems together and learn what it truly means when Gusteau said "Anyone can cook".

My opinion

I think that Ratatouille is one of the greatest Pixar films, every detail and character helps make the movie more interesting and fun to watch. Both main characters have father issues as Remy works hard to prove to his dad and Linguini works hard to live up to his father's status. The movie has been my favorite for a very long time, the animation is visually appealing and the characters can be very amusing as well. Overall, this is my favorite movie and I enjoy seeing it very much. It's something I recommend others to see as well.
